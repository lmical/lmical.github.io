## High-Order Methods

I am interested in high-order numerical methods for ordinary and partial differential equations.

In particular, I focus on arbitrary-high-order methods, e.g., Deferred Correction and ADER schemes.

## Structure-Preserving Methods

I develop numerical schemes that preserve important mathematical and physical properties of the underlying equations.

These include positivity-preserving schemes for enforcing physically admissible solutions, well-balanced methods for maintaining steady states, and asymptotic-preserving methods for accurately capturing asymptotic limits and resolving different physical regimes.

## Dual-Formulation Methods

I design methods based on the numerical solution of multiple formulations of the same governing equations to exploit their complementary strengths.

Applications include adaptivity, low Mach and low Rossby flows for the Euler equations and the thermal rotating shallow water equations, and multifluid flows.

## Adaptive Methods

I work on adaptive schemes able to dynamically select appropriate numerical discretizations.

Applications include efficient shock-capturing schemes and adaptive switching between implicit-explicit and fully explicit time integration methods.
